About ColorTube & Compliance Summary

ColorTube is an offline-capable casual liquid sorting puzzle game developed and published by Madhukar & Sons. We are committed to transparency, compliance with the Google Play Developer Program Policies, and adherence to global privacy laws.

Key highlights of our data practices:

  • Zero User Account Creation: No sign-up, email registration, password, or account profile is required to play.
  • Local Device Storage: Game progress, coins balance, and cosmetic unlocks are stored solely on your local device.
  • AdMob Monetization: Integrated Google Mobile Ads SDK serves adaptive banners, interstitials, and optional rewarded videos in compliance with Google Play Policies.
  • Child & Family Protection: ColorTube does not knowingly target or collect personal identifying data from children.
